How Bayesian optimization works here
Bayesian optimization
Choose the next expensive experiment using current evidence + uncertainty.
Gaussian Process
A probability distribution over plausible energy surfaces, not a second LLM.
Surrogate
The GP's cheap prediction of E(x) for configurations not measured yet.
Acquisition function
Expected Improvement balances promising low energy with uncertain regions.
The evidence panel uses the same run payload as dashboard/index.html: measured points, GP predictions, uncertainty, validation indicators, and the selected x*. No UI value is invented.
Where is the machine learning? After the seed configurations are measured, GaussianProcess.fit() learns an energy response surface from the observed configurations. Expected Improvement then selects the next configuration using the GP's predicted energy and uncertainty. The model is retrained after every BO evaluation.
